Everything you need to know.

How do I install Thirty APK on Android?
Tap the "Download APK" button. Once downloaded, tap the notification or open your Downloads folder, click Thirty-Pro.apk, and allow "Install Unknown Apps" when prompted.
Does Thirty work without internet connection?
Yes! Thirty is built on a 100% local-first IndexedDB and Capacitor architecture. All your habits, tasks, nutrition logs, and photos remain on your hardware even in airplane mode.
Are my photos and fitness data private?
Completely private. Photos and daily logs never leave your phone and are never sent to external servers or ad networks.
Do the home screen widgets work on modern Android?
Yes, Thirty provides native RemoteViews AppWidgets for Tasks and Habits designed specifically for Android 12 through Android 14+ with theme synchronization.
