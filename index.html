Assurance & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install the Thirty APK on my phone?
Click the "Download APK" button. When the file finishes downloading, tap the notification or navigate to your Downloads folder, tap Thirty-Pro.apk, and select "Install" (enabling "Install from Unknown Sources" if prompted by your browser).
Does Thirty function completely offline?
Yes. Thirty is engineered on an offline-first IndexedDB database. All your habits, tasks, nutrition records, timers, and photos remain fully accessible and functional with zero internet connection.
Are my body transformation photos secure?
100% private. Photos are compressed client-side and saved into your device's private sandboxed IndexedDB storage. They are never transmitted over the internet.
Do the home screen widgets stay updated?
Yes! Thirty provides native Android 12-14+ AppWidget providers with instant SharedPreferences broadcasting. Ticking a habit or completing a task in the app immediately refreshes your home screen widgets.
