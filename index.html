Clinical-grade nutrition, not just calorie counting

Most apps stop at calories and macros. Lock In tracks 43 individual bioactives — 13 vitamins, 9 minerals, omega-3/6 ratios, EPA, DHA, fiber, and more — with personalized RDA targets based on your age, sex, and activity level.

  • USDA and ICMR verified food database works entirely offline
  • Category adequacy scores detect chronic vitamin and mineral gaps
  • Quick-add frequently used foods for sub-5-second logging

Your data stays on your device. Period.

Lock In uses Dexie.js over IndexedDB — everything lives in your browser's local database. No accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no telemetry. Your weight logs, photos, and nutrition history never leave your phone.

  • Progress photos stored in sandboxed local storage, not a cloud bucket
  • Private vault with PIN protection for sensitive notes
  • Full data export so you always own your information

Real engineering, not a wrapper

Built with React 19, Vite 5, and Capacitor 8 for native Android performance. Focus timers use an Android Foreground Service so they keep running accurately even when the screen is off. Audio soundscapes are synthesized in real-time with the Web Audio API.

  • Zero timer drift during Doze mode and screen sleep
  • Client-side PDF generation with jsPDF — no server round-trips
  • Hardware camera integration with canvas-level photo compression

Designed for daily use, not just screenshots

Three visual themes (Light, Dark, Ultra-Dark) with 12 accent colors. Haptic feedback on check-ins. Drag-to-reorder tasks. Gamification with XP, levels, and milestone badges that reward consistency over perfection.

  • Executive PDF dossiers available in both light and dark themes
  • Weekly insights synthesize all 6 domains into a single discipline score
  • Recurring tasks and habits with flexible scheduling rules
